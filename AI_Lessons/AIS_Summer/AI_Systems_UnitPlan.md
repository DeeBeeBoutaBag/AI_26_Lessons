Unit Plan: AI Systems
=====================

**Course Title**
----------------

AI Systems

**Course Length**
-----------------

7 Weeks

**Lesson Frequency**
--------------------

5 lessons per week

**Lesson Duration**
-------------------

3 hours per lesson

**Unit Overview**
-----------------

AI Systems is a technical, project-based course where Geniuses learn how modern AI software actually works under the hood and how to turn that understanding into real software products. The course moves from foundational AI concepts into generative systems, computer vision, multimodal agents, and final capstone preparation. Throughout the unit, Geniuses explore how AI systems classify, generate, detect, track, reason, speak, and create.

This course is designed to feel like a mix of:

-   an AI lab
-   a software studio
-   a systems design course
-   a product build environment

Rather than only using AI tools, Geniuses learn how to think like builders of AI systems. They explore how inputs, outputs, models, data, workflows, trust, and user value come together inside real applications.

By the end of the unit, each Genius will have designed a serious AI software capstone concept and prepared the documentation, prototype plans, trust policies, and pitch materials needed to build and present it.

* * * * *

**Unit Essential Question**
===========================

How do modern AI systems see, learn, generate, reason, and act --- and how can Geniuses turn those capabilities into responsible software products?

* * * * *

**Unit Big Idea**
=================

AI systems are not magic. They are designed systems made of data, models, workflows, product choices, and trust decisions. Strong builders understand both the technical behavior of AI and the responsibility that comes with building it.

* * * * *

**Unit Objectives**
===================

By the end of this unit, Geniuses will be able to:

-   define and explain core AI system behaviors such as classification, generation, detection, tracking, and agent workflows
-   distinguish between traditional software systems and AI-powered systems
-   explain how AI systems learn from data through supervised and unsupervised approaches
-   build foundational AI workflows using Python and OpenAI tools
-   analyze how generative systems produce text, images, and multimodal outputs
-   explain how computer vision systems interpret images and video
-   design agentic AI systems that can see, reason, speak, and generate
-   identify trust, fairness, privacy, and review needs in AI software
-   document an AI software product with professional technical artifacts
-   prepare a capstone AI software app and venture concept for prototype build and final pitch

* * * * *

**Unit Standards / Competencies**
=================================

This unit supports development in:

-   Computational Thinking

-   Responsible AI Design

* * * * *

**Course Structure**
====================

Each lesson follows a consistent structure:

**1. Icebreaker**
------------------

A fun and engaging opening activity to wake Geniuses up and connect them to the day's topic.

**2. Lecture / Knowledge Section**
-----------------------------------

Slides, examples, guided discussion, and reflection to introduce the core technical ideas.

**3. Guided Practice**
-----------------------

A code-along or structured systems build in VS Code using Python, JavaScript, Node, OpenAI APIs, or related tools.

**4. Independent Practice**
----------------------------

Geniuses work individually on a mini-project or product-building task connected to the day's concept.

**5. Closure**
---------------

Share-outs, reflection, synthesis, and preview of the next lesson.

* * * * *

**Week-by-Week Unit Arc**
=========================

**Week 1 --- AI Systems Foundations, Ethics, and Responsible AI**
---------------------------------------------------------------

### **Theme**

What is an AI system? How do AI systems learn, classify, and make decisions responsibly?

### **Focus Topics**

-   what makes something an AI system
-   AI ethics and responsible AI
-   supervised learning
-   unsupervised learning
-   classification
-   rule-based systems vs AI-powered systems
-   first OpenAI API use

### **Key Understandings**

Geniuses learn that AI systems are not just tools that produce answers. They are technical systems with inputs, outputs, learning patterns, and consequences. They begin grounding their thinking in ethics, responsibility, and system behavior.

### **Mini-Project Direction**

Create an AI system concept brief that explains:

-   input
-   output
-   task type
-   learning type
-   trust concern
-   responsible design choice

* * * * *

**Week 2 --- Generative AI Systems**
----------------------------------

### **Theme**

How do AI systems generate text, images, and multimodal outputs?

### **Focus Topics**

-   what generative AI is
-   text generation
-   prompt design
-   image generation
-   multimodal generation
-   video generation concepts
-   synthetic media trust concerns

### **Key Understandings**

Geniuses move from systems that identify things to systems that create things. They learn that prompting is a form of system design and that generative systems are powerful but require strong structure, review, and trust thinking.

### **Mini-Project Direction**

Create a generative AI software concept that includes:

-   text, image, or multimodal generation
-   clear user value
-   prompt workflow
-   trust note
-   responsible design choice

* * * * *

**Week 3 --- Vision AI, Object Detection, and Sports Analytics**
--------------------------------------------------------------

### **Theme**

How do AI systems turn images and video into information?

### **Focus Topics**

-   computer vision foundations
-   image classification
-   object detection
-   bounding boxes
-   scene understanding
-   sports analytics
-   tracking
-   video vision
-   frame-by-frame analysis

### **Key Understandings**

Geniuses learn that visual AI is about more than recognizing pictures. It is about structured interpretation of scenes, motion, events, and patterns over time. They explore both the technical usefulness and the risks of visual AI.

### **Mini-Project Direction**

Create a vision-based AI software concept that explains:

-   visual input
-   task type
-   output
-   use case
-   trust concern
-   responsible design choice

* * * * *

**Week 4 --- Agentic AI That Can See, Talk, and Create**
------------------------------------------------------

### **Theme**

How do modern AI agents combine vision, reasoning, voice, and generation into one software workflow?

### **Focus Topics**

-   what makes a system agentic
-   vision + reasoning agents
-   text-to-speech and spoken output
-   image-generating agents
-   multimodal workflows
-   trust and authority concerns in agents

### **Key Understandings**

Geniuses learn that modern AI products increasingly combine multiple AI capabilities in one workflow. They design agents that feel more like software teammates than simple chatbots.

### **Mini-Project Direction**

Create a multimodal agent concept or prototype that includes:

-   user goal
-   visual input
-   spoken output
-   image generation purpose
-   trust concern
-   responsible design choice

* * * * *

**Week 5 --- Capstone Launch: Docs, Policies, Prototype Plans, and Pitching**
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

### **Theme**

How do you prepare an AI software capstone to be built and pitched seriously?

### **Focus Topics**

-   product vision
-   tech principles
-   README writing
-   technical docs
-   data use policies
-   trust and AI policies
-   MVP planning
-   capstone build roadmap
-   pitch and demo story

### **Key Understandings**

Geniuses shift from experimentation into product readiness. They prepare all the technical, product, and communication materials needed for a strong capstone build.

### **Mini-Project Direction**

Create a full capstone launch package with:

-   product vision
-   tech principles
-   README
-   technical doc
-   data policy
-   trust / AI policy
-   prototype plan
-   build roadmap
-   pitch
-   demo story

* * * * *

**Weeks 6--7 --- Capstone Build and Final Presentation**
-----------------------------------------------------

### **Theme**

Build, refine, document, and present a serious AI software app and venture concept.

### **Focus Topics**

-   prototype development
-   workflow refinement
-   feature prioritization
-   trust alignment
-   demo preparation
-   final pitch delivery

### **Key Understandings**

Geniuses apply all prior learning to create a real AI software capstone that reflects technical understanding, product thinking, and responsible AI design.

### **Capstone Direction**

Each Genius or team builds:

-   an AI software app
-   a business or venture concept around it
-   using one or more of:
    -   vision
    -   generation
    -   agents
    -   voice / TTS
    -   multimodal workflows

**Instructional Priorities**
============================

Across the whole unit, instruction should consistently prioritize:

**1. Real user value**
-----------------------

AI should solve a real problem for a real user.

**2. Systems thinking**
------------------------

Geniuses should understand how the system works, not just what it outputs.

**3. Responsible AI**
----------------------

Every week should include trust, fairness, privacy, and review thinking.

**4. Technical clarity**
-------------------------

Code, workflows, and documentation should be understandable and well-structured.

**5. Product relevance**
-------------------------

Lessons should connect to real software products and modern AI workflows.

**6. Capstone readiness**
--------------------------

Every week should feed forward into the final product build.

* * * * *

**Suggested End-of-Unit Capstone Outcomes**
===========================================

By the end of AI Systems, each Genius should be able to say:

-   I understand how modern AI systems work across text, image, vision, and agents.
-   I can explain the difference between classification, generation, detection, tracking, and multimodal reasoning.
-   I can design an AI software app around a real user problem.
-   I can document that app like a real builder.
-   I can define trust boundaries and data policies for the system.
-   I can pitch the product clearly and responsibly.

**Unit Closing Statement**
==========================

AI Systems helps Geniuses move from simply using AI to **understanding, designing, and building AI software systems**. By the end of the unit, Geniuses should not only know what modern AI can do. They should know how to shape it into products that are useful, technically thoughtful, and responsibly designed.
