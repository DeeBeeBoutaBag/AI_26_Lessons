Final Capstone Project Guidelines
=================================

**Purpose**
-----------

The Final Capstone is the culminating project for Applied AI. Each Genius will design, build, document, and present an AI venture that solves a real problem for a real user or community. This is not just a coding project. It is a venture, a product, and a professional presentation of your thinking.

* * * * *

**Capstone Goal**
-----------------

By the end of the capstone, each Genius must have:

-   a clear venture concept
-   a defined problem and target user
-   a working prototype
-   a business case
-   written documentation
-   a final presentation-ready demo

* * * * *

**Required Deliverables**
-------------------------

Each Genius or team must submit all of the following:

### **1. Venture Vision**

Must clearly explain:

-   venture name
-   problem being solved
-   target user or community
-   why the problem matters
-   why AI is useful in the solution
-   intended social impact

### **2. Business Model**

Must include:

-   value proposition
-   customer or user segments
-   channels
-   revenue or access model
-   key resources
-   key activities
-   key partners
-   cost structure

### **3. Working Prototype**

Must include:

-   a clear user flow
-   visible product value
-   at least one meaningful working interaction
-   clean design and readable structure
-   signs of iteration and improvement

### **4. Documentation**

Must include:

-   `README.md`
-   technical documentation
-   setup instructions
-   feature overview
-   roadmap or next steps

### **5. Final Pitch / Demo**

Must clearly explain:

-   what the product is
-   who it is for
-   what problem it solves
-   how it works
-   why it matters
-   why this venture should exist

* * * * *

**Strict Capstone Guardrails**
------------------------------

These rules are not optional.

### **1. Every capstone must solve a real problem**

-   No joke products
-   No random chatbot with no purpose
-   No vague "AI assistant for everything"
-   The problem must be specific and meaningful

### **2. Every capstone must be built for a real user**

-   "Everyone" is not a valid audience
-   The target user must be clearly defined
-   The venture must show awareness of real user needs

### **3. AI must be meaningfully used**

-   AI cannot be added just to sound impressive
-   The project must explain why AI improves the solution
-   If the same product works just as well without AI, that must be questioned

### **4. The capstone must be appropriate and responsible**

Projects may not include:

-   harmful, dangerous, or illegal use cases
-   cheating or academic dishonesty tools
-   products built to deceive, manipulate, or exploit people
-   fake engagement, fake popularity, or impersonation systems
-   products that encourage harm to self or others
-   projects involving illegal surveillance or unethical tracking
-   high-risk medical, legal, or financial claims without clear guardrails

### **5. The capstone must include trust and responsibility**

Every project must show:

-   at least one trust-building design choice
-   at least one responsible AI consideration
-   clear awareness of limits, risks, or uncertainty
-   no pretending the AI is perfect

### **6. The capstone must be original**

-   No copying another Genius's concept
-   No submitting a slightly renamed version of an existing famous startup
-   Inspiration is allowed, direct copying is not

### **7. The capstone must be complete enough to present**

Even if unfinished, it must still include:

-   a working prototype or strong partial demo
-   clear documentation
-   a clear explanation of what works now
-   honest explanation of what still needs work

### **8. The capstone must reflect effort across all areas**

A strong capstone is not just:

-   good code with no business thinking
-   a good pitch with no working product
-   pretty design with no real function
-   a strong mission with no execution

### **9. Every Genius must contribute**

If working in a team:

-   roles must be clearly defined
-   each Genius must contribute meaningful work
-   each Genius must be able to explain their role

### **10. Professionalism is required**

All submitted work must be:

-   school-appropriate
-   respectful
-   clearly written
-   organized
-   presentation-ready

* * * * *

**Explicit Rules**
------------------

Follow these exactly.

-   Your project must have a title.
-   Your project must have a one-sentence summary.
-   Your project must have a clearly defined target user.
-   Your project must have a real-world problem statement.
-   Your project must explain why AI is being used.
-   Your project must include a prototype.
-   Your project must include a README.
-   Your project must include a pricing, access, or sustainability idea.
-   Your project must include a final presentation.
-   Your project must show mission, function, and feasibility.

* * * * *

**Minimum Technical Expectations**
----------------------------------

Your capstone should use one or more of the following:

-   HTML
-   CSS
-   JavaScript
-   Node
-   NPM
-   OpenAI API
-   Python, when appropriate

Your capstone must not rely only on slides or only on written explanation.

* * * * *

**Minimum Presentation Expectations**
-------------------------------------

Your final presentation must answer:

1.  What is your venture?
2.  Who is it for?
3.  What problem does it solve?
4.  Why does this problem matter?
5.  Why is AI useful here?
6.  How does the product work?
7.  What makes the venture meaningful or different?
8.  What did you build?
9.  What still needs improvement?
10. What is your long-term vision?

* * * * *

**What Strong Capstones Usually Show**
--------------------------------------

-   a clear and meaningful problem
-   a specific user or community
-   thoughtful use of AI
-   culturally aware design choices
-   trust and responsibility
-   a believable prototype
-   strong written documentation
-   a realistic venture model
-   confident explanation and presentation

* * * * *

**Automatic Weaknesses That Will Hurt the Project**
---------------------------------------------------

These will lower the strength of the capstone:

-   vague audience
-   weak or unclear problem
-   AI added with no reason
-   no working interaction
-   no README
-   no business model thinking
-   no trust or policy awareness
-   copied idea with little originality
-   overpromising what the product does
-   no clear next steps

* * * * *

**Capstone Standard**
---------------------

A Final Capstone should feel like:

-   a real venture in progress
-   a product someone could understand
-   a solution designed for real people
-   a project built with care, logic, and responsibility

Not just:

-   a class assignment
-   a random demo
-   a flashy interface with no real substance

* * * * *

**Final Reminder**
------------------

The goal is not perfection.
The goal is to build something **real, thoughtful, useful, and ready to be taken seriously**.
