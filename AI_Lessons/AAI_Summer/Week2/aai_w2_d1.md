Lesson Plan: Applied AI Week 2, Day 1 --- How Modern Businesses Use AI and Planning an AI SaaS Product
====================================================================================================

**Duration:**
-------------

3 hours

**Lesson Overview:**
--------------------

In Week 2, Geniuses begin shifting from AI product thinking into AI product building for the web. Day 1 introduces how modern businesses incorporate AI into their products, services, internal workflows, and SaaS platforms. Geniuses learn that many of today's strongest companies do not just "use AI." They integrate AI into clear business functions like customer support, content generation, personalization, internal search, recommendation systems, workflow automation, and decision support.

This lesson also begins the Week 2 mini-project: building a real AI SaaS product experience. Because Geniuses have access to OpenAI API keys, the week will not stop at static websites. Instead, Geniuses will build toward a web-based AI product that includes business thinking, frontend design, backend logic, and real AI functionality. On Day 1, Geniuses will build the first version of an AI SaaS landing page using HTML and CSS, while also planning the product idea and feature they will eventually power with AI later in the week.

**Objectives:**
---------------

-   Explain how modern businesses incorporate AI into products, workflows, and SaaS offerings.
-   Identify common AI business use cases such as chat support, automation, personalization, summarization, and recommendation systems.
-   Distinguish between an AI feature and an AI business product.
-   Build a simple product landing page using HTML and CSS in VS Code.
-   Plan a real AI-powered SaaS concept that can later be connected to JavaScript, Node, and the OpenAI API.
-   Connect business value, user needs, and product messaging in a real website experience.

**Materials:**
--------------

-   Laptops for all Geniuses
-   VS Code installed
-   Internet access
-   Projector or display
-   Whiteboard and markers
-   Slide deck for lecture and discussion
-   Reflection journal or digital notes
-   Browser for viewing local HTML files
-   Starter folder for project files
-   OpenAI API key access for later week setup
-   Optional inspiration examples of SaaS landing pages

**Standards:**
--------------

-   Computational Thinking: Analyze how digital systems solve business problems through inputs, processes, and outputs.
-   Computer Science Practices: Design and develop a website that communicates a product clearly.
-   Web Development: Use HTML and CSS to structure and style a web page.
-   Digital Literacy: Evaluate how technology products communicate value and build trust.
-   Career Readiness: Apply communication, design thinking, and problem-solving to real-world business ideas.
-   Entrepreneurship and Innovation: Identify how AI can create business value through products and services.

**Lesson Activity:**
--------------------

### 1. **Introduction (Time: 25 minutes)**

-   **Objective:** Energize Geniuses and help them connect AI to real business use, not just chatbots or classroom demos.
-   Start with an icebreaker called **"What Business Would Use This AI?"**
-   This should be the first thing and the highest-energy part of the lesson.

#### **Icebreaker Option A: Physical Movement**

-   Label different areas of the room with business categories:
    -   **Education**
    -   **Health**
    -   **Retail**
    -   **Finance**
    -   **Entertainment**
    -   **Productivity**
-   Read AI use cases aloud and have Geniuses move to the category where they think the use case fits best.
-   Example prompts:
    -   AI that summarizes long documents
    -   AI that answers customer questions 24/7
    -   AI that recommends products based on browsing history
    -   AI that drafts marketing emails
    -   AI that helps users track habits
    -   AI that organizes meeting notes and action items
    -   AI that flags unusual spending activity
-   After each round, ask:
    -   Why would that industry want this?
    -   What business problem does this solve?
    -   Does it save time, make money, or improve user experience?
    -   What would users expect from this feature?

#### **Icebreaker Option B: Website Poll or Board**

-   Use Mentimeter, Slido, or a digital board.
-   Show AI use cases and let Geniuses vote on:
    -   Which industry would use it
    -   Whether it is a strong business use case
    -   Whether it feels like a product people would pay for
-   Discuss the most interesting or unexpected answers.

#### **Transition**

-   Say:
    -   "This week, we are moving from AI product ideas into actual web products. Businesses do not just think about AI. They package it, brand it, and present it."
-   Ask:
    -   What makes an AI tool feel like a real business?
    -   What makes someone trust a product enough to try it?

### 2. **Direct Instruction (Time: 50 minutes)**

-   **Objective:** Help Geniuses understand how AI appears inside modern businesses and how SaaS companies present their value to users.
-   Use slides to guide instruction and discussion.

#### **Suggested Slide Flow**

-   **Slide 1: Week 2 Focus --- AI in Modern Business**
    -   Businesses use AI to improve products, automate work, personalize experiences, and create new services.
    -   This week, Geniuses start building real websites for AI businesses.
-   **Slide 2: What Does It Mean to Incorporate AI Into a Business?**
    -   AI can be:
        -   the full product
        -   one feature inside a product
        -   an internal workflow tool
        -   a support system for employees or customers
-   **Slide 3: Common AI Business Use Cases**
    -   Customer support chat
    -   Content generation
    -   Summarization
    -   Recommendation engines
    -   Search and knowledge assistants
    -   Workflow automation
    -   Scheduling and planning
    -   Internal analytics and reporting
-   **Slide 4: What Is SaaS?**
    -   SaaS means Software as a Service.
    -   Users access software through the web.
    -   Businesses often charge monthly or yearly subscriptions.
-   **Slide 5: AI SaaS Examples**
    -   Writing assistants
    -   Study tools
    -   Meeting note summarizers
    -   AI research assistants
    -   AI planning and scheduling tools
    -   AI customer support systems
-   **Slide 6: Business Value of AI**
    -   Saves time
    -   Reduces manual work
    -   Improves quality
    -   Personalizes experiences
    -   Helps users make decisions faster
    -   Opens new revenue opportunities
-   **Slide 7: AI Product vs AI Feature**
    -   Product: the whole business is centered on the AI experience
    -   Feature: AI is one part of a larger product
    -   Ask: Which one is your idea becoming?
-   **Slide 8: What Makes a Good SaaS Website?**
    -   Clear headline
    -   Clear user problem
    -   Clear value proposition
    -   Strong call to action
    -   Visual trust
    -   Easy-to-scan sections
-   **Slide 9: The Stack We Can Build With**
    -   HTML and CSS for structure and design
    -   JavaScript for interactivity
    -   Node and NPM for backend setup
    -   OpenAI API for real AI functionality
    -   This means Geniuses can build more than a mockup
-   **Slide 10: Week 2 Mini-Project**
    -   Build an AI SaaS website
    -   Start with structure and messaging
    -   Later connect it to real AI behavior

#### **Discussion Questions**

-   How are businesses using AI in ways people may not even notice?
-   What makes an AI business useful enough for people to pay for?
-   What is the difference between a cool AI demo and a real SaaS product?
-   Why does a website matter for an AI business?
-   What would make you trust an AI SaaS product enough to sign up?

#### **Reflection Prompt**

-   Have Geniuses write for 4 minutes:
    -   "Describe one business or organization that could use AI better. What problem would AI help solve?"

### 3. **Guided Practice (Time: 60 minutes)**

-   **Objective:** Help Geniuses build a simple HTML and CSS landing page for an AI SaaS concept in VS Code while planning the real AI feature they will build later.
-   Explain that today's code-along is the first version of a real product website.
-   Geniuses will create a project folder and build the first version of a landing page.

#### **Guided Practice Build**

Create a folder called `ai-saas-starter`.

#### **Project Goal**

Build a landing page with:

-   A hero section
-   A product value proposition
-   A features section
-   A "how it works" section
-   A call-to-action button
-   Clean styling with CSS
-   A planned AI-powered feature section that previews what will be built later

#### **Setup Steps**

1.  Open VS Code.
2.  Create a folder called `ai-saas-starter`.
3.  Inside the folder, create:
    -   `index.html`
    -   `style.css`
4.  Open `index.html` with Live Server or in the browser.

### 4. **Independent Practice (Time: 30 minutes)**

-   **Objective:** Geniuses independently create the first version of their own AI SaaS landing page and plan the AI-powered feature they will build later in the week.
-   This begins the Week 2 mini-project that will continue through Day 4, with Day 5 as a flex and polish day.

#### **Mini-Project Title**

**AI SaaS Product Website --- Day 1 Foundation**

#### **Independent Task**

Each Genius creates a landing page for their own AI SaaS business idea.

Their page must include:

-   Product name
-   Headline
-   Short description
-   At least 3 features
-   A basic call-to-action section
-   A section describing one AI-powered feature they want to build later this week
-   Clean CSS styling

#### **Required Deliverables**

-   A working `index.html`
-   A working `style.css`
-   A product concept with:
    -   Product name
    -   Target user
    -   Problem solved
    -   3 features
    -   1 future AI feature
    -   One call to action
-   A short written reflection:
    -   What business problem does your SaaS solve?
    -   Why would someone want to use this product?
    -   What AI feature do you want to add later in the week?

#### **Facilitator Support Moves**

-   Help Geniuses narrow broad product ideas
-   Push them to make the headline clearer
-   Ask:
    -   Who is this website for?
    -   What problem does it solve right away?
    -   What would make someone click the button?
    -   What real AI feature could make this product stronger?
-   Encourage strong visual hierarchy and readability

### 5. **Closure (Time: 15 minutes)**

-   **Objective:** Reinforce the connection between AI business ideas, product communication, and future AI functionality.
-   Bring the class together for a final share-out.
-   Ask 3--5 Geniuses to show:
    -   Their product name
    -   Their headline
    -   Their target user
    -   One feature they are excited about
    -   One AI feature they want to build later this week
-   Lead a closing discussion:
    -   What makes an AI business idea feel real once it becomes a website?
    -   What was harder today: the business thinking or the website building?
    -   Why is product communication important in business?
    -   How does planning the AI feature now help later development?
-   Preview the next lesson:
    -   Geniuses will improve their website structure and start building interactivity so the product feels more real.

**Reflection / Takeaways:**
---------------------------

This lesson works best when Geniuses see that AI businesses are not just ideas in a notebook or prompts in a terminal. Real products need messaging, structure, presentation, and a plan for actual functionality. Day 1 of Week 2 helps Geniuses understand how AI becomes part of real business value and how websites help communicate that value. By the end of the lesson, Geniuses should feel that they are not just learning web development separately from Applied AI. They are using web development to package and present an AI business idea in a more real and useful form.

-   Remind Geniuses that this is the first version and it will improve throughout the week.
-   Reinforce that later in the week, the site can be connected to JavaScript, Node, and the OpenAI API.
