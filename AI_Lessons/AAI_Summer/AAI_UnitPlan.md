Unit Plan: Applied AI
=====================

**Course Title**
----------------

Applied AI

**Course Length**
-----------------

7 Weeks

**Lesson Frequency**
--------------------

5 lessons per week

**Lesson Duration**
-------------------

3 hours per lesson

**Unit Overview**
-----------------

Applied AI is a project-based course where Geniuses learn how to turn artificial intelligence into real products, ventures, and community-centered solutions. The course blends product design, entrepreneurship, technical prototyping, responsible AI, and business thinking. Geniuses move from understanding AI ethics and business opportunities into building actual AI-powered software ideas, designing user experiences, creating business models, and preparing a final capstone product and pitch.

This course is designed to feel like:

-   a startup studio
-   a product lab
-   a creative software workshop
-   a venture design experience

Rather than studying AI only as a technical tool, Geniuses learn how to apply AI in the modern world to solve real problems for real users. By the end of the unit, each Genius will have designed a serious AI software concept, built toward a working prototype, documented the product clearly, and prepared a capstone pitch and demo.

* * * * *

**Unit Essential Question**
===========================

How can Geniuses apply AI to build useful, responsible, and real-world software products and ventures?

* * * * *

**Unit Big Idea**
=================

Applied AI is about turning the power of AI into products that create real value. Strong AI ventures are not only technically interesting. They are rooted in real users, real problems, responsible design, and clear product vision.

* * * * *

**Unit Objectives**
===================

By the end of this unit, Geniuses will be able to:

-   explain how AI can be applied to real business, community, and product challenges
-   identify meaningful problems that AI software could help solve
-   analyze ethical and responsible AI concerns in product design
-   define clear users, use cases, and value propositions for an AI venture
-   build foundational AI-powered software workflows using tools like Python, JavaScript, Node, HTML/CSS, and OpenAI APIs
-   create product prototypes and software concepts that demonstrate real user value
-   design user-centered AI product experiences and interfaces
-   build business models and venture logic around AI software ideas
-   document AI software clearly through README files, technical docs, and policy documents
-   pitch and present an AI software venture with confidence and clarity

* * * * *

**Unit Standards / Competencies**
=================================

This unit supports development in:

-   Product Design
-   Entrepreneurship
-   Responsible AI
-   Technical Prototyping
-   User Experience Thinking
-   Business Model Design
-   Technical Communication
-   Software Documentation
-   Venture Pitching
-   AI Product Strategy

* * * * *

**Course Structure**
====================

Each lesson follows a consistent structure:

**1\. Icebreaker**
------------------

A fun and energizing activity to prepare Geniuses for the day's learning.

**2\. Lecture / Knowledge Section**
-----------------------------------

Slides, discussion, reflection, and examples to introduce the core concept.

**3\. Guided Practice**
-----------------------

A structured code-along, build activity, or product design exercise.

**4\. Independent Practice**
----------------------------

Geniuses work on a mini-project, prototype, or venture artifact tied to the lesson.

**5\. Closure**
---------------

Share-outs, synthesis, reflection, and next-step framing.

* * * * *

**Week-by-Week Unit Arc**
=========================

**Week 1 --- Business Ethics, Responsible AI, and Real-World AI Opportunity**
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

### **Theme**

What is Applied AI, and how do we build AI products responsibly?

### **Focus Topics**

-   what Applied AI means
-   AI in the modern world
-   real-world AI opportunities
-   business ethics
-   responsible AI
-   fairness, bias, trust, and misuse
-   product-centered problem identification

### **Key Understandings**

Geniuses begin by learning that Applied AI is not only about building with AI. It is about building responsibly, ethically, and with a real-world purpose. They identify meaningful problems and begin connecting AI to business and community needs.

### **Mini-Project Direction**

Create a responsible AI product concept brief that includes:

-   problem
-   user
-   why AI is useful
-   one ethical concern
-   one responsible design choice

* * * * *

**Week 2 --- AI in Modern Business and Early Product Building**
-------------------------------------------------------------

### **Theme**

How do businesses and startups use AI to create real products and services?

### **Focus Topics**

-   AI in startups and modern business
-   SaaS thinking
-   AI product categories
-   real-world business applications
-   HTML/CSS and web foundations
-   early website and product interface building
-   OpenAI API-powered product workflows

### **Key Understandings**

Geniuses explore how AI is used in real companies and begin building their own early AI product interfaces. They shift from concept thinking into software thinking and start seeing how AI can live inside a product.

### **Mini-Project Direction**

Create an early AI product website or interface that explains:

-   the product
-   the user
-   the use case
-   the AI-powered workflow
-   the value proposition

* * * * *

**Week 3 --- UI/UX, Culturally Rooted Products, and User Insight**
----------------------------------------------------------------

### **Theme**

How do we design AI products that feel useful, thoughtful, and rooted in real people?

### **Focus Topics**

-   user experience design
-   user interface design
-   culturally rooted product design
-   community-centered design
-   user insight and empathy
-   wireframing
-   prototypes
-   feedback and iteration

### **Key Understandings**

Geniuses learn that strong AI products do not succeed because they use AI alone. They succeed because they are designed around real users, real needs, and thoughtful experiences. This week strengthens their product sense and design thinking.

### **Mini-Project Direction**

Create a user-centered AI product prototype that includes:

-   user profile
-   problem context
-   product screens or flow
-   culturally rooted design choice
-   feedback notes and refinement ideas

* * * * *

**Week 4 --- Agentic AI, Multi-Agent Workflows, Data, Trust, and Policy**
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### **Theme**

How do modern AI products use agents, workflows, and trust-aware system design?

### **Focus Topics**

-   what agentic AI is
-   single-agent vs multi-agent workflows
-   routing and orchestration
-   data and trust
-   AI policy thinking
-   safety boundaries
-   product behavior and user trust
-   agent-based product concepts

### **Key Understandings**

Geniuses move into more advanced AI product patterns. They learn that modern AI products may include agent workflows, decision-making chains, structured outputs, and strong trust boundaries. They begin thinking more seriously about product architecture and policy.

### **Mini-Project Direction**

Create an agent-based AI product concept that includes:

-   user goal
-   agent workflow
-   data use
-   trust concern
-   one policy or review boundary

* * * * *

**Week 5 --- Radical Social Entrepreneurship, Business Models, Documentation, and Pitch Readiness**
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### **Theme**

How do we turn an AI product idea into a capstone-ready venture?

### **Focus Topics**

-   radical social entrepreneurship
-   value propositions
-   business models
-   business model canvas
-   prototyping for venture communication
-   README files
-   technical docs
-   subscriptions or pricing ideas
-   pitch preparation
-   capstone execution planning

### **Key Understandings**

Geniuses prepare to move into capstone build mode by turning their product idea into a venture package. They define business logic, write project documentation, clarify prototype scope, and prepare to explain the product clearly.

### **Mini-Project Direction**

Create a capstone launch package with:

-   venture vision
-   business model canvas
-   prototype
-   README
-   technical documentation
-   pricing/access model
-   short founder pitch
-   capstone build plan

* * * * *

**Weeks 6--7 --- Capstone Build and Final Presentation**
-----------------------------------------------------

### **Theme**

Build, refine, document, and pitch a serious Applied AI product and venture.

### **Focus Topics**

-   prototype development
-   feature refinement
-   user flow strengthening
-   venture polish
-   documentation updates
-   demo preparation
-   pitch practice
-   final presentation

### **Key Understandings**

Geniuses apply all prior learning to build a real AI software product and venture concept. The capstone demonstrates product thinking, business logic, technical creativity, documentation, and presentation skill.

### **Capstone Direction**

Each Genius or team builds:

-   an AI software product
-   a venture or business case around it
-   a prototype and product story
-   a final pitch and demo

-   working prototype or serious partial prototype

-   pricing/access logic
-   final pitch and demo

**Instructional Priorities**
============================

Across the whole unit, instruction should consistently prioritize:

**1\. Real user value**
-----------------------

AI products should solve meaningful problems for real people.

**2\. Responsible AI**
----------------------

Trust, ethics, fairness, and review should appear throughout the course.

**3\. Product thinking**
------------------------

Geniuses should think beyond features and toward workflows, usability, and value.

**4\. Venture thinking**
------------------------

Each product should connect to a real-world adoption path, business model, or social entrepreneurship angle.

**5\. Technical creativity**
----------------------------

Builds should be achievable but real enough to feel like actual software products.

**6\. Capstone readiness**
--------------------------

Every week should help Geniuses get closer to a serious final AI software venture.

* * * * *

**Suggested End-of-Unit Capstone Outcomes**
===========================================

By the end of Applied AI, each Genius should be able to say:

-   I can identify a real problem that AI could help solve.
-   I can design an AI product around a real user.
-   I can explain why AI is the right tool for the product.
-   I can build a prototype that shows the product workflow.
-   I can define the business or venture logic around the product.
-   I can document the product clearly.
-   I can pitch the product like a founder.

* * * * *

**Unit Final Deliverables**
===========================

By the end of the unit, each Genius should have:

-   a named AI product concept
-   a venture vision
-   a business model canvas
-   a prototype or serious partial prototype
-   a README
-   a technical doc
-   a pricing or access model
-   a pitch
-   a demo story or final presentation flow
-   a capstone build plan

* * * * *

**Unit Closing Statement**
==========================

Applied AI helps Geniuses move from curiosity about AI to the ability to **design, prototype, document, and pitch AI-powered products** that matter. By the end of the unit, Geniuses should not only understand what AI can do. They should be able to apply it to real-world needs with creativity, responsibility, and venture-minded thinking.
