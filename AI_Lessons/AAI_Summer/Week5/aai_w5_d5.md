Lesson Plan: Applied AI Week 5, Day 5 --- Subscriptions, Pricing, Pitching, and Capstone Launch Prep
==================================================================================================

**Duration:**
-------------

3 hours

**Lesson Overview:**
--------------------

Day 5 is the final guided lesson before Geniuses move into more independent capstone work during Weeks 6 and 7. This lesson brings together the venture thinking from the week by focusing on **subscriptions, pricing, pitch readiness, and execution planning**. Geniuses explore how mission-driven ventures can package value, choose sustainable pricing models, communicate their business clearly, and prepare to build with more independence.

The lesson helps Geniuses understand that strong ventures do not only have a mission, prototype, and documentation. They also need a realistic path for how the product could be offered, how users might pay or gain access, and how the founder can explain the venture with confidence. During guided practice, the class creates a simple **pricing and pitch package** in Markdown and builds a short capstone execution plan. During independent practice, each Genius defines a pricing model, writes a short pitch, and creates a plan for Weeks 6--7. By the end of the lesson, Geniuses should feel ready to step into capstone mode with more clarity, ownership, and structure.

**Objectives:**
---------------

-   Explain how subscriptions, pricing, and business packaging work in digital ventures.
-   Distinguish between free, freemium, subscription, licensing, and sponsored access models.
-   Identify a pricing or access model that fits a mission-driven AI venture.
-   Write a short founder-style pitch that clearly explains the venture's problem, value, audience, and vision.
-   Create a capstone execution plan for Weeks 6--7.
-   Reflect on how pricing, presentation, and planning support venture readiness.

**Materials:**
--------------

-   Laptops for all Geniuses
-   VS Code installed
-   Internet access
-   Projector or display
-   Whiteboard and markers
-   Slide deck for lecture and discussion
-   Reflection journal or digital notes
-   Existing venture files from Week 5 Days 1--4
-   Starter folder for pricing, pitch, and capstone planning files
-   Markdown preview in VS Code

**Standards:**
--------------

-   Computational Thinking: Organize venture ideas into clear execution, pricing, and communication structures.
-   Computer Science Practices: Prepare a technical product for continued development through planning and communication.
-   Technical Communication: Write clearly for users, judges, partners, and supporters.
-   Digital Literacy: Evaluate how digital ventures create value and structure access.
-   Career Readiness: Practice pitching, planning, self-management, and strategic communication.
-   Entrepreneurship and Innovation: Develop a realistic pricing approach, founder pitch, and capstone execution plan for a mission-driven AI venture.

**Lesson Activity:**
--------------------

### 1. **Introduction (Time: 25 minutes)**

-   **Objective:** Energize Geniuses and introduce the idea that building a venture also means deciding how people access it, support it, and understand its value.
-   Start with an icebreaker called **"Free, Freemium, or Paid?"**
-   This should be the first thing and the most fun thing to wake Geniuses up and get them ready for the lesson.

#### **Icebreaker Option A: Physical Movement**

-   Label three parts of the room:
    -   **Free**
    -   **Freemium**
    -   **Paid / Subscription**
-   Read venture scenarios aloud and have Geniuses move to the pricing model they think fits best.
-   Example prompts:
    -   An AI study support tool for high school Geniuses
    -   A school-facing AI platform for teacher workflow support
    -   A premium AI writing coach for college applicants
    -   An AI translation tool for families navigating school communication
    -   An AI volunteer management system for nonprofits
    -   A career readiness platform offered through youth programs
-   After each round, ask:
    -   Why should this be free?
    -   Why might freemium work here?
    -   Who would actually pay?
    -   How can access and sustainability both be protected?

#### **Icebreaker Option B: Pricing Debate**

-   Put venture scenarios on the board and ask small groups to choose a pricing model.
-   Each group must defend:
    -   who pays
    -   why they pay
    -   what stays free or accessible
-   Share out and compare models.

#### **Transition**

-   Say:
    -   "Pricing is not only about money. It is about how value is packaged, who gets access, and how the venture survives."
-   Ask:
    -   What should stay free in a mission-driven venture?
    -   What could be paid without hurting the mission?

### 2. **Direct Instruction (Time: 50 minutes)**

-   **Objective:** Help Geniuses understand pricing models, pitch structure, and capstone execution planning.
-   Use slides to guide instruction and pause often for discussion.

#### **Suggested Slide Flow**

-   **Slide 1: Day 5 Focus --- Package the Venture and Prepare to Build**
    -   Today is about pricing, pitching, and capstone readiness
-   **Slide 2: Why Pricing Matters**
    -   pricing shapes access
    -   pricing shapes sustainability
    -   pricing signals value
    -   pricing affects who can use the product and how the venture grows
-   **Slide 3: Common Digital Venture Pricing Models**
    -   **Free:** open access, often grant-supported, sponsor-supported, or mission-driven
    -   **Freemium:** core access is free, advanced features cost money
    -   **Subscription:** recurring monthly or yearly payment
    -   **Licensing:** schools, programs, or organizations pay for access
    -   **Sponsored Access:** a partner pays so users can access it for free
-   **Slide 4: Pricing Questions Founders Should Ask**
    -   Who gets the most value?
    -   Who can realistically pay?
    -   What should stay accessible?
    -   What premium feature or service creates sustainable value?
    -   How does pricing support the mission instead of hurting it?
-   **Slide 5: Mission-Driven Pricing**
    -   social impact and sustainability can work together
    -   examples:
        -   school pays, Geniuses use free
        -   freemium for individuals, licensing for organizations
        -   sponsored access for underserved communities
-   **Slide 6: What Makes a Strong Pitch**
    -   clear problem
    -   clear audience
    -   strong value proposition
    -   why now
    -   why this matters
    -   what makes the venture different
    -   what the next step is
-   **Slide 7: Short Pitch Structure**
    -   What is the venture?
    -   Who is it for?
    -   What problem does it solve?
    -   Why is it important?
    -   Why is this the right solution?
    -   What is the vision?
-   **Slide 8: Capstone Readiness**
    -   a strong capstone plan includes:
        -   what you are building
        -   what still needs to be done
        -   your priorities
        -   what success looks like by the end of Week 7
-   **Slide 9: Build Planning Questions**
    -   what is already working?
    -   what is unfinished?
    -   what is your highest priority next?
    -   what needs to be built first?
    -   what can wait?
-   **Slide 10: Day 5 Goal**
    -   create a pricing model, short pitch, and capstone plan so your venture is ready for independent development

#### **Discussion Questions**

-   Why is pricing part of venture design, not just math?
-   What pricing model best supports mission-driven work?
-   What makes a short pitch feel strong?
-   Why is it important to know what you will build next before capstone starts?
-   What should a founder be able to explain clearly in under one minute?

#### **Reflection Prompt**

-   Have Geniuses write for 4 minutes:
    -   "What access model and pricing model best fit your venture, and why?"

#### **Sample `pricing_and_pitch.md`**
```
# Pricing and Pitch: BridgePath AI

## Pricing Model
BridgePath AI will use a mixed model:
- Free access for individual Geniuses to core exploration tools
- School and youth program subscriptions for premium features, dashboards, and educator support
- Sponsored access opportunities through partners

## Why This Model Fits
This model keeps core access open for Geniuses while allowing schools and organizations to support the long-term sustainability of the platform.

## What Is Free
- Basic career exploration
- Introductory AI support
- Initial next-step guidance

## What Could Be Premium
- Program dashboards
- Educator tools
- Advanced personalization
- Opportunity tracking features
- Reporting and progress tools

## Short Pitch
BridgePath AI is a mission-driven AI career support platform designed for high school Geniuses, especially those who may not already have access to strong professional networks or mentorship. The venture helps Geniuses explore career paths, prepare for opportunities, and organize their next steps with accessible, personalized guidance. By combining mission-driven access with sustainable school and program subscriptions, BridgePath AI aims to make career readiness more equitable and more actionable.
```
#### **Sample `capstone_plan.md`**
```
# Capstone Plan: BridgePath AI

## Venture Goal for Weeks 6--7
Build a stronger capstone prototype that clearly shows how Geniuses can explore careers, receive AI-supported guidance, and leave with useful next steps.

## Current Strengths
- Clear mission and user focus
- Strong venture vision
- Business model canvas completed
- Prototype started
- README and technical documentation created

## Main Priorities for Capstone
1\. Improve the prototype flow
2\. Make the AI interaction feel more realistic
3\. Strengthen the value proposition on the product page
4\. Add better personalization or guidance features
5\. Prepare for final presentation and demo

## What Needs to Be Built or Improved
- Cleaner prototype interactions
- Better result formatting
- Stronger demo experience
- More polished visuals
- More complete documentation

## Minimum Goal by End of Week 7
A clear, functional capstone prototype with a strong mission, business logic, documentation, and presentation-ready story.

## Stretch Goal by End of Week 7
A more polished prototype with stronger interaction, clearer personalization, and a more confident final pitch.
```

### 4. **Independent Practice (Time: 30 minutes)**

-   **Objective:** Geniuses independently create a pricing model, short pitch, and capstone plan for their venture.
-   This completes the Week 5 mini-project and prepares for capstone weeks.

#### **Mini-Project Title**

**Capstone Venture Package --- Day 5 Pricing, Pitch, and Launch Prep**

#### **Independent Task**

Each Genius creates:

-   `pricing_and_pitch.md`
-   `capstone_plan.md`

Their work must define:

-   how the venture creates access
-   who might pay or support it
-   what the short founder pitch is
-   what their capstone priorities are for Weeks 6--7

#### **Required Deliverables**

-   A completed `pricing_and_pitch.md`
-   A completed `capstone_plan.md`
-   Optional `pitch_prompts.js` or helper file if useful
-   A venture package that includes:
    -   pricing or access model
    -   free vs premium or sponsor-supported thinking
    -   a short pitch
    -   a clear capstone execution plan
-   A short written reflection:
    -   Why does this pricing model fit your venture?
    -   What part of your pitch feels strongest?
    -   What are your top priorities going into capstone weeks?

#### **Facilitator Support Moves**

-   Help Geniuses choose a pricing model that fits both mission and reality
-   Ask:
    -   Who could realistically support this venture financially?
    -   What should remain accessible?
    -   What part of the pitch should be clearer?
    -   What are the most important 2--3 things to build next?
-   Encourage confident but realistic capstone planning

### 5\. **Closure (Time: 20 minutes)**

-   **Objective:** Reinforce that strong ventures need access thinking, strong communication, and a clear execution path.
-   Bring the class together for a final share-out.
-   Ask 3--5 Geniuses to share:
    -   their venture name
    -   their pricing or access model
    -   one line from their pitch
    -   one major priority for Weeks 6--7
-   Lead a closing discussion:
    -   What makes a pricing model mission-aligned?
    -   What makes a short pitch effective?
    -   Why does capstone planning matter before building independently?
-   Preview next phase:
    -   Geniuses will move into more independent capstone work with their venture package, prototype, docs, and plan in hand.

**Reflection / Takeaways:**
---------------------------

This lesson works best when Geniuses understand that a venture is not ready only because it has a product idea or a prototype. It also needs a way to be explained, sustained, and built forward. Day 5 helps Geniuses leave the guided portion of the course with a fuller venture package and a stronger sense of ownership. By the end of Week 5, they should feel that they have something real enough to keep building with greater independence and purpose during capstone weeks.

**Facilitator Notes**
---------------------

-   Keep using **Geniuses** consistently in examples and discussion.
-   Reinforce that pricing should support access and sustainability together.
-   Encourage short pitches that are specific and grounded.
-   Help Geniuses focus on realistic capstone priorities instead of trying to do everything at once.
-   Frame this day as a launch point, not an ending.

------------------

-   A venture prototype
-   A GitHub-ready README
-   A technical documentation file
-   A pricing or access model
-   A short founder pitch
